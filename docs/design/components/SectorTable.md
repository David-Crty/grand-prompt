# SectorTable

Sector times for one lap per car (here lap 4), coloured by the motorsport convention, with the ideal lap (sum of best sectors).

**Provide**: `classification` laps, `lap` (or `best`), `entries`.

- Purple `timing-fastest`: fastest of anyone so far. Green `timing-personal`: the car's own best. Yellow `timing-slower`: slower than its own best. Each cell carries the time, and a title names the colour, so colour is never alone.
- Retired cars are left out of sector comparisons.

Maps to `<SectorTable>`.
