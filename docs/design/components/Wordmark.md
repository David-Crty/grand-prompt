# Wordmark

The Grand Prompt mark (a corner chevron followed by a prompt cursor, read as `>_`) and the name set in Archivo Expanded 800.

**Provide**: `size` (`s` 11 px in the tower head, `m` 15 px in page headers, `l` 28 px on cards and video). Colour is `text-100`; the mark inherits it.

- Use the horizontal lockup everywhere; the stacked lockup (asset) only in square spaces such as the social card.
- The favicon and app icon are the mark in chalk on an `ink-000` tile with `radius-2` corners.
- Do not tint the mark with an entry or signal colour, outline it, or put it on a photo without a `hud-panel` behind it.
- Clear space: one cursor height on every side.

Maps to `<Wordmark size>` in `packages/ui/brand`.
