# CaveatBanner

The honesty line for results that are not a fair ranking: trials, unequal budgets, unfixed effort, a weak house driver.

**Provide**: `kind` (`trial`, `budget`, `effort`, `reference`) and the sentence, plus a link to the explanation.

- Place it above the result it qualifies, on the event page, the classification overlay, the share card and the video's final card (short form).
- One sentence, stating the fact: "Reasoning effort was not fixed: Codex builds ran at low or medium, Claude builds at the CLI default."

Maps to `<CaveatBanner>`.
